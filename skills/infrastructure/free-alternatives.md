# Cost Optimization and Alternatives

## Goal
Replace expensive components with lower-cost managed or self-hosted alternatives while preserving reliability targets.

## Workflow
1. List costly components and monthly spend share.
2. For each component, provide 1-3 alternatives.
3. Compare by cost, operational burden, migration effort, and risk.
4. Recommend phased migration order based on ROI and risk.

## Comparison Table
| Component | Current Cost | Alternative | Cost Delta | Ops Overhead | Migration Risk |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|---|

## Rules
- Keep recommendations stack-agnostic unless constraints demand a specific ecosystem.
- Do not propose self-hosted options without calling out operational trade-offs.
- Include a rollback option for each migration.
